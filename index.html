The beginning of a legacy

A story written by generations.

On 24 October 1964, Zambia emerged as an independent republic. What followed was a journey shaped by ambition, political change, economic transformation and the resilience of its people. Every era left its mark on the nation we know today.

The defining chapters of modern Zambia.

CHAPTER 01
1964

The Birth of a Nation

On 24 October 1964, Northern Rhodesia became the independent Republic of Zambia. Kenneth Kaunda became the nation's first President. Independence marked the beginning of a new national identity and the challenge of building a sovereign state.

CHAPTER 02
1991

The Return of Democracy

After years under a one-party political system, Zambia returned to multiparty democracy. The 1991 election brought Frederick Chiluba and the Movement for Multiparty Democracy into power, marking a historic political transition.

President Frederick Chiluba
Vice President Levy Mwanawasa
A new democratic chapter
CHAPTER 03
2021

A New Generation of Change

The 2021 general election brought Hakainde Hichilema and the United Party for National Development to government. The peaceful transfer of power became another defining moment in Zambia's democratic journey.
